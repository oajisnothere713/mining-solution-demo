Operational Challenges

Key challenges in explosives
field operations today

Field operations in the explosives and mining industry involve complex, time-sensitive coordination across multiple teams, vehicles and locations — often in remote areas with limited connectivity.

📋

Scheduling Complexity

Managing MMU vehicles, crew, shotfirers and surveyors across multiple blast sites — single-day, multi-day and recurring jobs — requires real-time coordination that manual systems struggle to support reliably.

📦

Inventory Visibility Gap

Knowing the real-time stock position of materials like AN, ANE and detonators — across depot, in-transit MMUs and consumed at the blast hole — is operationally critical but difficult without a connected system.

📄

Delivery Documentation

Capturing actual quantities delivered at the blast hole, getting customer sign-off and translating that into ERP transactions and customer invoices is a multi-step process that benefits enormously from digitalization.

🔌

Remote Connectivity

Mine sites and quarries are often in areas with limited or no cellular connectivity. Any field solution must be designed to work offline and sync automatically when connectivity is restored.

🔗

ERP Integration

Field operations data — deliveries, stock movements, goods receipts — needs to flow accurately and in real time into core ERP systems for inventory accuracy, compliance and invoice generation.

👤

Customer Transparency

Mining customers benefit from real-time visibility of scheduled deliveries, confirmed quantities and signed delivery dockets — enabling faster invoice approval and stronger operational partnerships.

The Transformation

How operations change
with digitalization

A step-by-step view of how each stage of field operations is transformed — from planning through to billing.

Auto-switching in 6s
01

Manual Scheduling

Blast schedules, vehicle assignments and crew rosters are managed on whiteboards, spreadsheets or via informal communication. Any change requires manual re-coordination across multiple teams.

Coordination overhead
02

Informal Communication

Schedule updates are shared via phone, email or messaging apps to drivers and field crew. Version control is a challenge — field teams may work from outdated information.

Risk of miscommunication
03

MMU Departs Without Digital Record

The truck leaves with printed or verbal instructions. There is no live connection between the field vehicle and the depot or ERP system during operations.

No real-time visibility
04

Paper Delivery Docket at Site

The operator records actual quantities on a paper docket at the blast hole. The customer signs a physical copy. This paper document is then transported back to the depot.

Data capture delay
05

Manual ERP Entry

Office staff manually enters docket data into the ERP system — often the next day. This creates a lag in inventory updates, stock reconciliation and billing initiation.

24–48 hour delay
06

Delayed Invoice Processing

Invoice is raised days after delivery. Quantity discrepancies between planned and actual require manual resolution. Customer payment cycle starts later than necessary.

Cash flow impact
01

Digital Scheduling Board

Planner creates blast bookings — single day, multi-day or recurring — on a week-view scheduling board. Vehicle availability, crew assignment, products and blast IDs are all captured digitally with a full audit trail.

Complete traceability
02

Automated Notifications

Field operators receive job details on their mobile app. Customers receive an automated SMS or email confirming the scheduled delivery — the day before. No manual communication required.

Zero manual comms
03

Mobile App — Offline Ready

The MMU operator works from the mobile app at the blast site. The app functions fully offline in areas with no connectivity. All data is securely stored on the device and synced when connected.

Works in remote areas
04

Digital Delivery Docket

The operator enters actual quantities pumped at the blast hole. The customer signs digitally on the device. The delivery docket is immediately locked, stored and linked to the job record.

Instant, tamper-proof record
05

Automatic ERP Update

On network reconnection, the app syncs to the core ERP system. Goods receipt is posted automatically. Inventory is updated in real time. No manual data entry required at any stage.

Real-time accuracy
06

Same-Day Invoice Generation

Invoice is generated automatically from the signed digital docket. The customer views and approves it through the customer portal. The billing cycle begins the same day as delivery.

Accelerated cash flow

AI-Powered Forecasting & Planning

A rolling 6-month forecast engine that automatically generates planned material orders based on scheduled blast jobs — giving procurement and supply chain teams advance visibility with no manual spreadsheet work.

  • →Planned orders created directly in the ERP system — procurement works from live system data, not emails
